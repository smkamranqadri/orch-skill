# Orch Model

Research current model availability, pricing, plans, and relevant measurements for the requested
jobs. Ask the owner for missing preferences, draft the matrix in the format in
`references/matrix.md`, and write it to `~/.config/orch/matrix.md` only after approval. Do not
store research inputs in the matrix.
